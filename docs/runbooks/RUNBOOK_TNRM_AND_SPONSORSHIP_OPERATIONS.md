# Operational Runbook: TNRM, Rehabilitation & Sponsorship Management

**Scope**: Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for the **Hope For Strays UM** team to manage field TNRM data, Rehabilitation House operations, personalized monthly sponsor perks, and shelter needs.

---

## 🐾 1. TNRM (Trap-Neuter-Return-Manage) Operations

### Protocol:
1. **Intake & Identification**:
   - Every animal trapped on campus (Universiti Malaya) or surrounding PJ zones is assigned a record.
   - Clinical ear-notching (universal symbol for neutered community animals) is performed during spay/neuter surgery.
2. **Post-Op Monitoring**:
   - Animals recover at the Rehabilitation House for 2–5 days post-surgery before release to their registered colony feeding station.
3. **Colony Management**:
   - Regular health checks, rabies/core vaccination boosters, and feeder coordination.

---

## 🩺 2. Rehabilitation House Operations & Status Tracking

### Status Lifecycle:
```
[Rescue Intake] ──► [In Rehabilitation] ──► [Veterinary Clearance] ──► [Available for Adoption]
                             │
                             └──► (If wild/feral colony animal) ──► [TNRM Returned & Managed]
```

### Logging Updates on Pet Profiles:
- **Frequency**: Minimum once weekly per animal in rehabilitation.
- **Update Content**:
  - Date & recovery phase (e.g. "Week 3: Cast removal and physical therapy").
  - Clinical progress notes (weight, medication response, socialization score).
  - High-resolution photo or short video clip.

---

## 💖 3. Personalized Animal Sponsorship Fulfillment (RM30 Tier)

### Sponsor Perks Management:
1. **Monthly Photo & Video Progress Dispatches**:
   - On the 1st of each month, export active sponsors for each pet from the database or admin dashboard.
   - Dispatch a curated WhatsApp / Email update featuring the animal's latest photos, recovery status, and personal caregiver note.
2. **Arranging Occasional Sanctuary Visits**:
   - Sponsors may request a 30-minute supervised visit session during weekend sanctuary visiting hours (Tue–Sun, 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM).
   - Require 48 hours prior notice via WhatsApp to ensure caregiver availability and animal comfort.
3. **Certificate of Sponsorship**:
   - Automatically generated with the donor's name, dedicated pet's name and photo. It is a recognition certificate, not a tax receipt: the shelter holds no LHDN approval (#239).

---

## 📦 4. Rehabilitation House Wishlist & Needs Updating

### Category Breakdown:
1. **Urgent Needs**: Supplies with less than 3 days of stock remaining (e.g., specific post-op wet food, surgical gauze, F10 disinfectant).
2. **Regular Needs**: Daily recurring supplies (puppy kibble, pee pads, fleece blankets).
3. **Long-term Improvements**: Facility upgrade equipment (modular stainless steel kennels, quarantine canopy).
4. **TNRM Equipment**: Field trapping gear (humane drop traps, transfer cages, bite-proof gloves).
